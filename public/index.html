01Why speed

Spot freight goes to whoever answers first.

A request lands as an email, a spreadsheet or a scanned PDF. Someone rebuilds it against the tariff, adds surcharges and margin, then types up the quote. On a small desk, that is the same few people every time, and every hour of delay is a load that might go elsewhere.

  • 217min

    Mean response time to a freight RFQ, across a survey of 70 firms. The median was 30 minutes.

    Magaya, 2023

  • 57hrs

    Average time for a manual quote from large forwarders in a mystery shopper test. Many never replied at all.

    Freightos, 2018

  • <10staff

    Headcount at most of the roughly 1,800 UK forwarders in BIFA. Quoting competes with everything else.

    BIFA

Published industry research, not QuotePilot customer results.

02How it works

From inbox to booking, on one route.

Four stops. QuotePilot does the heavy lifting in between, and the draft waits at stop three until a person on your team says go.

  1. Stop 01

    Intake

    Reads quote requests from email, Excel and PDF, alongside the rate sheets and tariffs you already use. It maps a customer's spreadsheet onto your template and uses OCR on rate sheets, bills of lading, customs documents, invoices, POs, packing lists and transport orders.

  2. Stop 02

    Draft the quote

    Prices the job from your tariffs, surcharges and margin rules. Road, sea and air each keep the rating basis your team already works with.

  3. Stop 03

    Your team approves

    Nothing reaches a customer without your approval. Someone on your desk checks the draft, adjusts if needed and hits send.

  4. Stop 04

    Booking

    When the customer says yes, QuotePilot carries the accepted quote straight into a booking, without retyping the details.

06FAQ

Good questions.

Anything else, email cathal@autopilotconsultants.com.

Who is QuotePilot for?

Small and mid-size European freight forwarders and 3PLs. Specifically operations managers, owners, and the sales or quoting team who answer rate requests every day.

What can it read?

Incoming quote requests in email, Excel and PDF, plus your rate sheets and tariffs. It can map a customer's Excel file onto your template. OCR covers rate sheets, bills of lading and master bills, customs documents, invoices, purchase orders, packing lists and transport orders.

Which freight modes are covered?

Road (FTL, LTL and groupage, with pallet, loading metre, weight band and zone tariffs, minimum charges and chargeable weight), sea (LCL by weight or measure, FCL per container, and surcharges such as BAF, CAF and ETS) and air (chargeable weight). Extras include tolls, fuel, waiting time, extra stops, tail lift and urgency.

Will a quote ever go out on its own?

No. Nothing goes to a customer without the forwarder's approval. A person on your team reviews every draft and sends it.

What does the pilot involve?

A handful of forwarders, starting with one repeat lane or tariff each. Together we measure the time from request received, to quote sent, to booking. QuotePilot is pre-launch, so pilot partners help shape it.

Who is behind it?

Autopilot Consultants, based in Hamburg. Email cathal@autopilotconsultants.com.

What happens to my details if I join?

We store them so we can contact you about the pilot, and nothing else. The privacy note lists exactly what is kept and how to have it deleted.
