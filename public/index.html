Why speed

Spot freight goes to whoever answers first.

A request lands as an email, a spreadsheet or a scanned PDF. Someone checks the tariff, adds surcharges and margin, then types up the quote. On a small desk that's the same few people every time, in between everything else. It's fiddly, repetitive work, and it's exactly the kind an assistant can take off your plate.

  • 217min

    Mean response time to a freight RFQ, across a survey of 70 firms. The median was 30 minutes.

  • 57hrs

How it works

Four steps from inbox to booking.

QuotePilot will do the reading, the looking up and the sums. Your team makes the call at step three.

  1. Step 1

    Intake

    QuotePilot will read quote requests from email, Excel and PDF, alongside the rate sheets and tariffs you already use. It will map a customer's spreadsheet onto your template and use OCR on scans, covering rate sheets, bills of lading, customs documents, invoices, POs, packing lists and transport orders.

  2. Step 2

    Draft the quote

    It will price the job from your tariffs, surcharges and margin rules. Road, sea and air each keep the rating basis your team already works with.

  3. Step 3

    Your team approves

    Someone on your desk checks the draft, adjusts it if needed and approves it with a click. During the pilot, nothing goes to a customer without that approval.

  4. Step 4

    Booking

    When the customer says yes, QuotePilot will carry the accepted quote into a booking, so nobody retypes the details.

    Who is QuotePilot for?

    Small and mid-size European freight forwarders and 3PLs. In particular owners, operations managers and the sales or quoting team who answer rate requests every day.

    What will it read?

    Incoming quote requests in email, Excel and PDF, plus your rate sheets and tariffs. It will be able to map a customer's Excel file onto your template. OCR will cover rate sheets, bills of lading and master bills, customs documents, invoices, purchase orders, packing lists and transport orders.

    Which freight modes will be covered?

    Road (FTL, LTL and groupage, with pallet, loading metre, weight band and zone tariffs, minimum charges and chargeable weight), sea (LCL by weight or measure, FCL per container, and surcharges such as BAF, CAF and ETS) and air (chargeable weight). Extras will include tolls, fuel, waiting time, extra stops, tail lift and urgency.

    Will a quote ever go out on its own?

    Not during the pilot, and never unless you choose it. Today and throughout the pilot, QuotePilot works in co-pilot mode, so a person on your team approves every quote before it's sent. Autopilot mode is a future option for hands-off quoting. It will be off by default, and it will only run if you switch it on.

    What does the pilot involve?

    A handful of forwarders, starting with one repeat lane or tariff each. We set QuotePilot up on it with you, and together we measure the time from request received, to quote sent, to booking. QuotePilot is pre-launch, so pilot partners help shape it.

    Who is behind it?

    Autopilot Consultants, based in Hamburg. Email cathal@autopilotconsultants.com.

    What happens to my details if I join?

    We store them so we can contact you about the pilot, and for nothing else. The privacy note lists exactly what is kept and how to have it deleted.
